About me

I'm a software developer and consultant. I've been delivering commercial software since the mid-'90s, mostly in .NET and Python, and I help teams design systems that stay understandable as they grow.

Pascal apologised for writing a long letter because he didn't have time to write a short one. Software has the same problem: teams write more code because constraints leave no room to write less. I'm a software simplifier. That usually means fewer moving parts, clearer boundaries, and delivery that stays straightforward.

I currently work as a Principal Product Software Engineer at Duende Software, the makers of IdentityServer. Before that, I spent nearly a decade at Particular Software, the makers of NServiceBus. Before Particular, I spent almost two decades building software in financial services. I'm a recognised speaker at developer conferences around the world.

What I do

  • System design and architecture: finding service boundaries and keeping systems easy to change.
  • Development: C#/.NET and Python, including libraries, analyzers, and tooling for other developers.
  • User authentication: sign-in, user management, and authentication for .NET applications.
  • Build and delivery: CI/CD, build pipelines, and versioning, with an emphasis on keeping things simple.
  • Training and speaking: conference talks and workshops, including a two-day Modern SOA workshop.

I've worked with DDD, CQRS, event sourcing, messaging, and microservices, and I've seen what helps and what adds complexity.

For consulting, training, or workshop enquiries, get in touch on LinkedIn.
