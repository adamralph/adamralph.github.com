Privacy

This site uses GoatCounter to count page views. GoatCounter does not use cookies or persistent identifiers and does not collect personal data. It records aggregate page views, referrers, browser, operating system, screen size, and country. The data is used only to understand which pages are read. It is not sold or shared.

There is no consent banner because GoatCounter does not use cookies or track visitors across sites.
